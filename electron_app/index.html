A small, living ritual

Grow a garden with your breath.

Breathe Garden turns live breathing and pulse signals into a quiet landscape. It is a creative experience, not a medical device.

01

You choose when the camera turns on

The camera stays off until you press Start. A visible Stop camera button is always available while the session is running.

02

Your video is not stored by this app

The Presage SmartSpectra SDK reads camera frames to estimate breathing rate and pulse. Breathe Garden does not save video, images, or raw frames.

03

A summary stays in memory

When you stop, you can keep a short session summary in memory, save it locally on this device, or discard it. Nothing is saved by default.

What happens to my data?

The SmartSpectra SDK contacts Presage to validate your API key and subscription. This app sets enableTelemetry: false to opt out of the SDK's aggregate telemetry. Even with telemetry off, SDK authentication and service validation may involve network metadata such as your IP address, app credentials, attestation data, hashed device identifiers, and SDK version. Breathe Garden does not call AI Insights and does not send your metric values to Presage itself.

Breathe Garden keeps only an in-memory session summary by default. If you choose to save a summary, it is stored in this app's local storage on this device and can be deleted at any time. The app does not store video, images, or raw camera frames. Read the Consent & Data Handling section in the README for the full disclosure.
