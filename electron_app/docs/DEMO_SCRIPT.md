# Breathe Garden — 60–90 second demo script

## Before recording

- Add a valid `SMARTSPECTRA_API_KEY` to `.env` and restart the app.
- Use a real camera session. Do not use simulated mode.
- Close other camera apps.
- Sit near soft, even light with one face centered and upper body visible.
- Start a test session before recording to confirm breathing and pulse become stable.
- Clear saved sessions if you want a clean first frame.
- Have a backup recording ready in case the live signal is poor.

## Shot list and voiceover

### 0:00–0:08 — Title and consent

**On screen:** Breathe Garden consent panel. Camera is off. The Start button is disabled until the consent checkbox is checked.

**Voiceover:**

> “This is Breathe Garden. It turns live breathing and pulse into a generative landscape. The camera stays off until I choose to start.”

### 0:08–0:18 — Consent and start

**On screen:** Check the consent box, press **Start breathing garden**, and let the camera preview appear.

**Voiceover:**

> “I consent to the camera processing this session. The app never saves video or raw frames, and I can stop the camera at any time.”

### 0:18–0:35 — Calibration

**On screen:** Preview and calibration panel. Show the signal hint. Let the breathing and pulse cards settle.

**Voiceover:**

> “SmartSpectra estimates breathing rate and pulse. While the signal settles, the app shows guidance instead of pretending the numbers are ready. Breathing typically needs about thirty seconds; pulse settles sooner.”

### 0:35–1:00 — The living landscape

**On screen:** Stable session. Show the garden responding: slow sky and growth with breathing, small light pulses with pulse. Point out the live values and confidence bars.

**Voiceover:**

> “Once the signal is stable, the garden responds. My breathing shapes the slow movement of the sky and the growth around the garden. My pulse adds smaller pulses of light. Low-confidence or stale data relaxes the scene instead of driving it.”

### 1:00–1:15 — Stop and summary

**On screen:** Press **Stop camera**. Camera preview disappears. Summary dialog appears.

**Voiceover:**

> “When I stop, the camera turns off and the session becomes a small summary in memory. Nothing is saved unless I choose to save it.”

### 1:15–1:25 — Privacy and close

**On screen:** Show the summary dialog and the privacy details, then close.

**Voiceover:**

> “Breathe Garden uses only breathing and pulse, not facial expressions. SDK telemetry is disabled, but the SDK still authenticates with Presage. It is a creative experience, not a medical device.”

## Backup line if the live signal is poor

> “The garden is designed to stay calm when the signal is uncertain. It waits for a fresh, stable reading rather than inventing one.”

## Do not say

- “This measures your health.”
- “This diagnoses stress.”
- “No data leaves your device.”
- “This is medical-grade.”
- “The SDK is fully offline.”
